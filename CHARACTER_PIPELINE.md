# WAR OF NYX — Character overhaul (Section 40)

This document tracks the move from the jointed placeholder characters to skinned, animated anime warriors. It covers what was wrong, the target art direction, six design sheets, the animation architecture, the asset pipeline, how to import real models, and how the result is checked.

**Status (2026-10-16): phase B (KAI) is implemented with procedural art, and the framework (phase C) is in place.**
- **Done for KAI:** a real skinned humanoid mesh, a Unity Humanoid Avatar, 26 authored humanoid clips and a Mecanim controller. It runs in game for the human player, the bots and the menu preview.
- **Still procedural:** KAI's mesh and textures are generated by code, not modelled by an artist. Read the honest limits below.

---

## 1. Diagnosis: why the old characters looked like stick figures

| What we had | Why it read as a "stick figure" | What replaces it |
|---|---|---|
| 16–28 **separate rigid meshes**, one per joint (`CharacterModels` + `CharacterStyler`) | No skinning: limbs were tubes stacked at the joints, with visible creases and gaps at the shoulders, elbows, hips and knees whenever they bent. The silhouette was segmented, like a mannequin. | **One continuous skinned mesh** per detail level (`KaiModel`, `MeshKit.Skinning`). Joint weights blend over 3–7 cm (`NyxSkeleton.Chain`), so elbows and knees bend smoothly. |
| A plain transform tree (`CharacterBody`): no Avatar, no Animator | Nothing could play an animation clip or retarget one. | A **Unity Humanoid skeleton** (`NyxSkeleton`): hips, spine, chest, upper chest, neck, head, shoulders, arms, hands with five three-joint fingers, legs, feet and toes, plus helper bones for hair and coat. A validated **Avatar** is built by `AvatarBuilder`. |
| **No animation clips**; one procedural gait (`CharacterAnimator`) shared by everyone | Every character walked identically, and actions were transform wobbles. | **Authored humanoid clips** in muscle space, retargetable to any humanoid. They are shaped by a per-character `MotionStyle`. A Mecanim controller with blend trees and layers plays them. |
| Faces were discs on an ellipsoid, and hair was thin tubes | Eyes read as "two circles", and the hair as spikes. | A **sculpted head** (jaw, cheekbones, nose bridge and tip, brow ridge, eye sockets, ears) with smooth anime face shading. A **painted face atlas** gives almond eyes with layered irises, lashes, brows, nose and mouth, in 8 eye, 4 brow and 4 mouth expressions. **Layered hair clumps** with anisotropic highlights sway on helper bones. |
| Vertex-coloured toon material only | Materials were flat. | The NyxToonVC material system (fabric weaves, leather, metal, polymer, lacquer, hair, glowing circuits) and the `NyxFace` decal shader. |

The old jointed path still exists for **AKIRA, YUNA and REN** until their skinned models are made in phase D.

## 2. Art direction: WAR OF NYX dark-fantasy anime

- **Mood:** elite supernatural hunters in a futuristic survival tournament. The look is dangerous, stylish and heroic.
- **Shapes:**
  - Adult anime proportions, about 7.5 heads tall. Long legs, defined shoulders, a natural neck.
  - Each silhouette must read from 30 m, even from behind: long coat, heavy armour, flowing hair.
- **Faces:**
  - Sharp, V-shaped jaws on the men and softer jaws on the women.
  - Large but proportionate almond eyes, with a heavy upper lash line and a flick.
  - The iris is a layered gradient with a dark limbal ring and two highlights.
  - The nose and mouth are minimal (anime convention).
- **Colours:**
  - Black, midnight blue, violet, cyan, crimson and silver, with gold used sparingly.
  - Each character owns one energy colour.
  - Enemies stay readable: bright accents on dark outfits, and a rim light on every character.
- **Shading:**
  - Two-band cel lighting with tinted shadows, plus restrained outlines that are thin at close range and grow slowly with distance.
  - Material-specific highlights.
  - Emissive accents only where the design calls for them.
- **Not allowed:** copying any existing anime character's face, outfit or emblem. The reference anime guides only the broad qualities: contrast, anatomy and energy.

## 4. Animation architecture (implemented)

```
Character root (PlayerMovement, hit shapes, WeaponVisualRig)       ← gameplay, authoritative
 └─ Visual
     └─ KAI_Model (prefab)  Animator(Humanoid Avatar, controller) + LODGroup
         ├─ Armature/Hips/… (65 bones: humanoid + hair + coat helpers)
         ├─ KAI_LOD0/1/2  SkinnedMeshRenderer (body: NyxToonVC, face: NyxFace)
         ├─ CharacterAnimationController  parameters, weapon pose, hand IK, look-at, footsteps
         ├─ CharacterFootIK               ground placement after the Animator
         ├─ CharacterFacialAnimator       blink, expressions, eye glow (tile switches)
         ├─ CharacterSecondaryMotion      hair and coat springs, leg collision
         └─ CharacterVisualEffects        spawn burst, strike sparks, victory aura
```

- **Profiles:** each character has two ScriptableObjects.
  - `CharacterVisualProfile` (Resources/WarOfNyx/Characters/{NAME}): the prefab, face submesh, energy colour and art source.
  - `CharacterAnimationProfile`: the controller (an override of the shared one), weapon carry offsets, recoil body language, spine aim share, idle-variation interval and foot IK range.
- **Controller** (`CharacterControllerBuilder`):
  - **Base layer** (IK pass):
    - 2D freeform-directional **Locomotion**: idle, walk and run in four directions. Playback is scaled by `LocoRate` so the feet match the ground speed.
    - **Sprint**, a **Crouch** 1D blend, **Air**, **Slide** and **Seated**.
    - **Death** and **Victory** (any-state), and an **IdleVariant**.
  - **Upper-body layer** (avatar mask: body, arms, head, fingers):
    - The 7 melee combo attacks. `MeleeRate` re-times each clip so its strike frame lands when the server's windup ends.
    - The **Hit** reaction.
- **Gameplay stays authoritative:**
  - Locomotion is **in place**, and root motion is off.
  - The movement model moves the character; the clips only follow it.
  - Melee damage timing comes from the shared rules, never from the clip.
- **Weapons:**
  - **Firearms:** placed in front of the body (hip, aim, sprint carry, reload, healing, recoil). Both hands are solved onto their grips after the Animator, so guns never float.
  - **Blades:** ride in the animated right hand; the combo clips swing them.
- **Look-at:** the spine and head follow the aim pitch, and the head follows free look.
- **Foot IK:** a two-bone solve on the animated legs after the Animator, plus a hip drop.
- **Why IK runs after the Animator:** generated humanoid clips carry no IK-goal curves, so Unity's goal IK would pull the feet to the body centre. The look-at needs no goals and stays in the IK pass. This was found and fixed during the preview QA.
- **Bots:** they use exactly the same loader and controller, so an AI-controlled KAI moves like KAI. Bot intelligence is a separate system.
- **Online:** not implemented (Fusion is not installed). When it is, nothing new needs replicating: the controller rebuilds animation from the replicated movement state and combat events (fired, swing, hurt, defeated, victory) that are already networked for the old characters.

## 5. Asset production plan, and honest limits

### What exists now (procedural, phase B)
`CharacterAssetBuilder` (run by project setup) generates the following into `Assets/Generated` (git-ignored, rebuilt from code):
- Three skinned LOD meshes on one skeleton.
- The painted face atlas, as PNG with ASTC compression on Android.
- The face material and a validated Humanoid Avatar.
- 26 humanoid clips, the controller and the upper-body mask.
- The runtime prefab and both profiles.

The clips are **real humanoid muscle-space clips**. Each frame is posed on the skeleton with IK, read back with a `HumanPoseHandler` and written as muscle curves, so they retarget to an artist-made humanoid later.

### What this environment cannot produce
- **Artist-quality sculpted meshes and hand-painted textures.** No 3D tools are installed here (no Blender, Maya or VRoid Studio), and I can't sculpt by hand. KAI's mesh is built from mathematical surfaces. It is a coherent, skinned and animated anime character, but not "high-budget anime" quality.
- **Motion-captured or keyframed-by-hand animation.** The clips are authored from code (gait solvers, keyed poses). They are readable and personal, but not as nuanced as an animator's work.
- **Licensed assets** need your account or decision. That includes Mixamo animations (Adobe account), store character bases, and any VRoid / VRM export.

### The production path to final art (needs you or an artist)
1. **Model** each character in **Blender** (free) or **VRoid Studio** (free, anime-specific; exports VRM).
   - Follow the design sheets above and the T-pose bind described in §6.
   - VRoid models come with a humanoid rig, face blend shapes and hair spring bones.
2. **Texture:** use a hand-painted anime style. Keep the face atlas layout, or use blend shapes.
3. **Rig:** use a humanoid skeleton with the same bone set (§6), plus hair and coat helper bones.
4. **Animate:** keep the generated clips, or replace slots with licensed or mocap clips through an `AnimatorOverrideController`. The slot names are in §4.
5. **Import** following §6, then set the prefab on the character's `CharacterVisualProfile`. Nothing else in the game changes.

## 6. Model import guide (artist FBX / VRM)

1. **Export:**
   - **Axes and scale:** FBX, Y up, Z forward, metres (scale 1), T-pose bind with palms down.
   - **Size:** feet at y = 0, crown at about 1.8 m.
   - **Skinning:** at most 4 influences per vertex.
2. **Import settings:**
   - **Rig:** Animation Type **Humanoid**, then **Configure**. Map every required bone, plus fingers and toes.
   - **Model:** read/write off, Normals **Import**, Blend Shapes on (faces).
   - **Animation:** **Bake Into Pose** for root rotation, Y and XZ (in-place).
3. **Materials:**
   - Body uses `WarOfNyx/NyxToonVC`, which reads uv2 = (material id, occlusion, outline weight, weave). Assign uv2 in Blender, or use a per-material variant.
   - The face uses `WarOfNyx/NyxFace` (atlas tiles), or blend shapes driven by `CharacterFacialAnimator`.
4. **Prefab:**
   - **Root components:** Animator (Humanoid Avatar) and a LODGroup with three or more levels.
   - **Hierarchy:** the SkinnedMeshRenderers sit under the root.
   - **Helper bones:** keep the names `HairBack1/2`, `HairSideL/R`, `HairFront`, `CoatFrontL1/2`, `CoatBackL1/2` and so on, so secondary motion finds them.
5. **Profile:** put a `CharacterVisualProfile` named exactly like the roster name (e.g. `KAI`) in `Resources/WarOfNyx/Characters/`.
6. **Hand axes:** if the imported hands are not "fingers along X, palm down" in the bind pose, update `CharacterAnimationController.HandFrame` and `MeleeGripRotation` for that model.

## 6b. Supplied glTF models (`ArtSource/Characters`, automatic import)

Drop a `.glb` into `ArtSource/Characters/` (git-ignored: third-party art is never committed), add a line to `ImportedCharacterCatalog` (roster slot, file, height, rig source, motion style, outfit text), then run project setup or **War of Nyx > Characters > Import supplied character models**. `ImportedCharacterBuilder` does the rest and writes `Assets/Generated/Characters/{SLOT}/{SLOT}_ImportReport.txt`:

1. **Read** the file (`GlbReader`, `SourceModel`): meshes, materials, embedded textures, skin, credit (Sketchfab writes title, author, licence and source into the file). glTF is right-handed, so X is mirrored and winding reversed.
2. **Normalise:** feet at y = 0, hips over the origin, facing +Z, scaled to the catalogue height. Duplicated vertices from exporter splits are welded.
3. **Rig:**
   - **File has a skeleton** (`RigSource.EmbeddedSkin`; Character Creator / AccuRIG, Mixamo, VRoid names): `SkinConverter` maps every joint to the shared skeleton (twist, share, facial and breast helpers merge into their parent part), keeps the file's weights and raises an A-pose to the T-pose with those weights.
   - **No skeleton** (`RigSource.AutoTPose`): the model must stand in a T-pose. `HumanoidFitter` measures the joints from cross-sections (armpits, wrists, crotch, ankles, the ten fingers from the separated finger tips and the webs between them). `AutoSkinner` computes weights: distance to each bone over that body part's thickness, region rules (a left-leg bone never owns the right thigh, hair stays on the head), surface smoothing per position (copies of a seam vertex share weights, or the seam would crack).
4. **Detail levels:** Unity's Mesh LOD generator (`MeshLodUtility`) simplifies the dense source. Each level reuses the previous level's vertices, so UVs and weights carry over exactly.
5. **Materials:** URP Lit. Base colour as imported (4096 px on PC, ASTC 6×6 at 2048 or 1024 on Android); glTF metallic-roughness converted to URP metallic-smoothness.
6. **Avatar, motion, prefab, profiles:** a validated Humanoid Avatar on the fitted skeleton; the character's movement style (`MotionStyle.Agile`, `Heavy`, …) authored on the KAI rig and retargeted through the Avatar; weapon carry scaled by body size; the CHARACTERS screen shows the model's outfit and its credit.

A missing file leaves the slot's previous look. Models the importer cannot rig are refused with the reason, never faked.

**Supplied 2026-10-17, removed the same day at the owner's request.** Four Sketchfab files were audited. Android 18 (2M triangles, unrigged T-pose) was auto-rigged as YUNA's look and the Tripo astronaut (Character Creator rig) was converted as REN's; both worked in game (tests, studio and in-game captures) and were then **deleted**: the catalogue is empty, the copies in `ArtSource/` are gone, and setup removes any imported look whose file is no longer catalogued, so YUNA and REN are back to their previous looks. The Mai Shiranui model (posed, unrigged) and 3DAISTOCK (vertex colour only, CC-BY-NC) were never imported. The pipeline stays for licensed or original models.

## 7. Naming conventions
- **Bones:** `Hips`, `Spine`, `Chest`, `UpperChest`, `Neck`, `Head`, `{Left|Right}Shoulder`, `…UpperArm`, `…LowerArm`, `…Hand`, `…{Thumb|Index|Middle|Ring|Little}{1|2|3}`, `…UpperLeg`, `…LowerLeg`, `…Foot`, `…Toes`, plus the helper bones above.
- **Assets:** `{NAME}_LOD{n}.asset`, `{NAME}_Avatar.asset`, `{NAME}_FaceAtlas.png`, `{NAME}_Face.mat`, `{NAME}.controller`, `{NAME}_UpperBody.mask`, `Anim/{NAME}_{Slot}.anim`, `{NAME}_Model.prefab`, `{NAME}.asset` (profile).

## 9. Android budgets (provisional; not yet profiled on a device)

| Level | Target (section 40.16) | KAI now | Used |
|---|---|---|---|
| LOD0 | 15–25k triangles | **24,984** | < ~10 m (screen height > 16 %) |
| LOD1 | 7–12k | **13,636** (slightly over) | ~10–35 m |
| LOD2 | 2–5k | **4,721** | beyond, culled below 0.15 % |

- **Rig and textures:** 65 bones (GPU skinning). Four bone weights at LOD0, two at LOD1 and LOD2. One shared body material and a 1024² face atlas (ASTC 4×4).
- **Animator:** `CullUpdateTransforms`.
- **Distance limits:** foot IK up to 30 m, secondary motion up to 35 m, faces up to 40 m. Faces update every 0.4 s beyond 15 m.
- **Reduced motion:** `CharacterSecondaryMotion.Quality` (0 off, 1 hair only, 2 full).
- **Not measured yet:** real-device frame time, skinning cost and memory with 24 characters on screen.

## 10. Visual acceptance test

**Automated (EditMode, `SkinnedCharacterTests`):**
- Every required humanoid bone is mapped, with 30 finger bones, in a T-pose about 1.8 m tall.
- Every vertex is skinned, the weights sum to 1, and each level stays within its triangle budget and is lighter than the one before.
- Every triangle faces outward, and the elbows blend two bones.
- All five face parts are present, all 16 expression tiles are painted, a blink closes the eye, and wide eyes show more than narrow ones.
- After setup: the Avatar is humanoid and valid, there are 3 LODs, and every clip slot is filled with humanoid clips.

**Rendered (editor):** `War of Nyx > Characters > Render KAI previews` (batch: `CharacterPreviewRender.BuildAndRenderKai`). It renders front, three-quarter, side and back views, two face close-ups, and 17 clip frames with a katana in hand. It also logs the authored and the retargeted joint positions, which must match.

**In game:** `WarOfNyx.exe -devCapture <folder> -devCaptureOnly roster|characters`.

**Reject the character if any of these is true:**
- It looks segmented or stick-like.
- The face is just circles.
- The hair is a single lump.
- The feet slide, or it goes into a T-pose.
- Weapons float outside the hands.
- The coat cuts deep into the legs.
- Two characters share a walk.
